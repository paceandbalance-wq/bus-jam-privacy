# Bus Jam privacy policy

> **DRAFT.** This policy is still being written and is not final. It describes how the
> Bus Jam app will handle your data; details in [square brackets] are still to be filled in.

**Last updated:** [date]

## Who we are

Bus Jam is made by [developer / company name] ("we", "us"). We are responsible for your
personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).
Contact: [email address].

## The short version

- You don't need an account. The game signs you in anonymously so it can save your progress.
- We store your game progress (levels, stars, fares, lives) so you don't lose it.
- We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we never store a phone number.
- Ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward. Google shows them, and in the UK
  and EU we ask for your consent before any personalised ads.
- You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

## What we collect and why

**Game progress.** Your current level, levels saved for later, best stars per level,
fares (in-game coins), lives, trophies, and when you last earned a reward or refilled
lives. We use it to run the game and save your progress. Legal basis: necessary to provide
the game you asked for (contract).

**A random player ID.** When you first open the game we create an anonymous account with
a random ID. It links your progress to your device. It is not your name or email.

**Rewarded-ad counts.** How many reward ads you've watched today and when, so we can give
you an ad-free day after 5. Legal basis: contract.

**If you sign in with Google or Apple** (not offered at the moment): your email address,
name and, from Google, your profile photo, to identify your account. Legal basis: contract.

**Technical data.** Like most apps, the services we use receive your IP address and basic
device information (model, operating system, app version) when the app connects to them.
We use this to run the service securely, stop cheating and fake apps (Firebase App Check),
and see how quickly the app loads and responds (Firebase Performance Monitoring). Legal
basis: our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure and working well.

**Advertising.** If you choose to watch a reward ad, Google AdMob shows it. AdMob may
collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact
with the ad. In the UK and EU we ask for your consent before any personalised ads; if you
say no, you may still see non-personalised ads. Legal basis: consent (personalised ads)
or legitimate interests (non-personalised ads). You can reset or limit your advertising ID
in your phone's settings.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and we don't take
payments.

## What is stored on your device

The app keeps a few things on your device: copies of your fares and lives, whether sound
is on, today's ad count, your sign-in session, and a cached copy of your progress for
offline play. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. (Clearing app data
also removes your anonymous sign-in, so your saved progress can't be recovered.)

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use these service providers, who process data for us or
under their own terms:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Performance
  Monitoring): sign-in, saving your progress, security and performance.
- **Google AdMob**: reward ads (only if you choose to watch one).
- **Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple**: only if you use them.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Where it is stored

Your game data (your player record) is stored by Google Cloud in the United States
(Firestore location `nam5`). Sending personal data from the UK to the US is allowed under
the **UK–US data bridge** (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), which
Google LLC is certified under. For the EU/EEA, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework applies,
and Google's Standard Contractual Clauses are there as a back-up.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how
we use it, and to withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time. In the app: Settings > Privacy options (ad consent)
and Settings > Delete my data. Or email [email address] with your player ID [shown in Settings]. If
you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's
Office (ico.org.uk).
